o A wide range of conditions were investigated:
13% < O2 < 30%
5% < CO2 < 75%
5% < He < 45%
1 < D0 < 4.5 mm
o Diffusive extinction due to water absorption, and 
radiative extinction were observed. The limiting oxygen 
index (LOI) ~ 12-13% oxygen at atmospheric pressure.
o Simplified theoretical models (Nayagam, 2013), and 
numerical simulations (Farouk & Dryer,2012) explain 
experimental observations reasonably well.
