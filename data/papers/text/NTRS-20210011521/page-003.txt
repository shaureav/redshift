The sample cards contained three or four samples and were configured for four ignitions. As will be discussed below, 
two samples on Saffire IV and one sample on Saffire V were not ignited. The conditions for the five successful tests 
are presented in Table 1 and the sample card layout is shown in Figure 2. The tests were conducted in a rectangular 
flow tunnel that was 30 cm high by 45 cm wide by 109 cm long.  The ends of the ducts were equipped with flow  
straighteners that connected to plenums . The inlet and outlet ducts  connected to these plenums and were parallel  to 
the flow duct producing a folded geometry. These ducts enabled flow mixing  and temperature and measurement  of 
the concentrations of oxygen, carbon dioxide and carbon monoxide on the inlet and outlet. The flow was induced by 
a fan in the inlet duct and measured by a vane anemometer at the entrance of the inlet duct. The samples were affixed
