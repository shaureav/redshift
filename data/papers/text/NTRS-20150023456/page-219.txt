Figure 189.—Test FLEX–186. Free-floating methanol droplet burning in a 0.23/0.52/0.25 O2/N2/CO2, 
0.70-atm ambient environment. The droplet drifted south in the High-Bit-Depth Multispectral (HiBMs)

diffusively.
