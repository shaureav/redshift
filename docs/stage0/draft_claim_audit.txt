4. IN THE DRAFT, MISSING FROM THE BRIEF, AND A JUDGE WOULD EXPECT IT
========================================

1. Hypoxia layer.
   - DRAFT p2 has a line of equal O2 partial pressure as one of the three risks. BRIEF E3 (L196-204) lists no hypoxia layer. BRIEF A1 mentions hypoxia only as part of the EAWG rationale.
   - This matters numerically. My arithmetic: 8.2 x 0.34 = 2.79 psi ppO2, against 14.7 x 0.21 = 3.09 psi, so the points are not on the same iso-line.

2. SoFIE, FM2 and FLARE.
   - DRAFT p3 checks the planner against SoFIE's 10 to 40% / 0.2 to 1.5 atm range, and p4 names the experiment planners for SoFIE, FM2 and FLARE.
   - The BRIEF planner (L156) has no facility ranges, although L252 cuts a "facility filter" that is never specified.

3. Material and atmosphere status badges.
   - DRAFT p10 defines four badges: Tested here, Tested nearby, Not tested, Unknown.
   - REVIEW p5 keeps "a status badge". The BRIEF has only data-quality flag badges.

4. Prepared answers to judges' hard questions.
   - DRAFT p17-18 has three: is the gap PSI-only, why not predict, did the AI come up with the idea.
   - REVIEW p2 praises them. The BRIEF has no Q&A preparation.

5. Evidence for "explains, does not predict".
   - DRAFT p3 gives the 273 FLEX tests and the 86/88% and 70/68% accuracies.
   - BRIEF L41 asserts the conclusion but has no golden check or test that reproduces these numbers.

6. "Below 9 psi only 28 tests" (DRAFT p2). A strong context number that is absent from the BRIEF golden checks.

7. The SAME/SAME-R smoke particle-size vs ISS detector question.
   - This is DRAFT p2 Detection. The BRIEF only lists SAME papers (L166) and a cuttable "material detection section".

8. "Is the gap PSI-only" and retrieval-date scoping (DRAFT p17). The BRIEF covers the retrieval timestamp but not an explicit scope statement in the UI.

Not in the DRAFT either, but a judge would expect it (from GUIDE L584, L629): a statement that all evidence is from microgravity or 1 g, and PSI has no partial-gravity (Moon 1/6 g) data. Neither the BRIEF nor the DRAFT says this.
